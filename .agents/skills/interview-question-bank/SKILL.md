# Skill: Resume-Wide Interview Question Bank

Use this skill when implementing or editing the CareerOS interview question bank.

1. Read `docs/17_INTERVIEW_QUESTION_BANK_AND_SKILL_TAXONOMY.md` and `seed-data/interview-skills-catalog.json` first.
2. Preserve the distinction between DSA coding problems and conceptual/practical skill interview questions.
3. Support both skill-first and topic-first browsing.
4. Each question must have a primary skill, topic, type, difficulty, priority and source/origin.
5. Do not claim a question was asked by a named company without a verifiable source.
6. Do not copy full proprietary problem statements or paid editorial solutions.
7. Make the bank useful with AI disabled. AI generation/evaluation belongs to Phase 4 and must be user-triggered.
8. Show content coverage gaps instead of fake placeholder questions.
9. Keep progress (status, confidence, notes, revisions) user-owned and protected by RLS.
10. Verify filters, search, detail view, answer notes, status, revision, persistence and mobile layout.
